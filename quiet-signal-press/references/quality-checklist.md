# Quality Checklist

- Vertical 3:5 canvas unless the user requested another ratio.
- At least 72% reads as open paper rather than filled illustration.
- Exactly one dominant signal cluster; no inventory of decorative objects.
- Signal has a concrete material carrier and one readable visual relation.
- One saturated accent remains visible at thumbnail size.
- Paper, print, scan, clip, or stamp behavior is visible.
- No ad hierarchy, logo, CTA, glossy mockup, 3D, or dense collage drift.
- Supplied photos have a declared role and retained invariants.
- Reference-only images do not contribute exact text, brands, signatures, watermarks, or layouts.
