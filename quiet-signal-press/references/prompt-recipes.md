# Prompt Recipes

## Generate recipe

```text
Create a single 3:5 vertical paper editorial poster about <SUBJECT OR IDEA>.

Use a warm <PAPER TONE> field with visible fiber, quiet scan grain, dry printed texture, and 72–88% calm open space. Place one compact visual signal at <POSITION>, occupying about <SIZE>% of the canvas: <CONCRETE CARRIER>. The signal expresses <ONE RELATION OR TENSION>, not a full scene.

Use one decisive <ACCENT COLOR> accent inside the signal and restrained charcoal, warm gray, or faded neutral support. Keep the image flat and materially printed: clipped paper, stamped ink, halftone, soft xerox residue, or imperfect press registration. Typography is <TEXT MODE>; if present it is tiny, partial, and non-promotional.

Avoid full-bleed photo, advertisement layout, logo, CTA, glossy render, cinematic light, 3D, neon, dense collage, generic pictograms, brand packaging, and long clean readable text.
```

## Text-free switch

Append this sentence when text is not wanted:

```text
Generate no readable characters, numbers, labels, logos, captions, signatures, watermarks, or typography-like marks.
```

## Prompt-only response

Return the completed prompt, the selected recipe, the visual signal, the accent color, and a short avoid list. Do not claim an image was generated.
