# Visual System

## Fixed contract

- **Canvas:** 3:5 vertical by default.
- **Field:** warm white, clay, smoke-gray, or pale flax paper; visible fiber, dry ink, press grain, faint scan wear, and matte absorption.
- **Attention:** 72–88% of the frame remains visually open. One signal cluster occupies roughly 7–20%.
- **Signal:** a single concrete carrier—cropped photograph, stamped silhouette, folded paper shape, ink fragment, specimen, small object, clipped illustration, or interrupted relation.
- **Accent:** one saturated hue appears decisively within the signal cluster. A secondary hue is allowed only as a trace.
- **Type:** optional, extremely sparse, and treated as an archival index or imperfect fragment; never a polished headline. Omit typography when the user asks for text-free output.
- **Surface:** flat printed/scanned material world; no deep mockup shadows, glossy render, or cinematic staging.

## Variable recipes

Choose one per output:

1. **Margin specimen:** a small observed object near one edge with a colored stamp behind it.
2. **Interrupted pair:** two related fragments separated by a narrow empty interval.
3. **Clipping window:** a photo or illustration crop peeking through an irregular paper aperture.
4. **Folded mark:** one torn or folded color form carrying a tiny object or symbol.
5. **Index trace:** a faint route, contour, or repeated mark leading to one small focal object.

## Avoid

Full-bleed photography, commercial product hierarchy, CTA, logos, branded packaging, generic icon sets, glossy mockups, 3D, neon, cyberpunk, thick frame borders, busy scrapbook layering, clean dashboard UI, fashion-magazine drama, or long readable paragraphs.
